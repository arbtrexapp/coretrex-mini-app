How It Works

Coretrex provides a digital finance platform where users can fund their accounts, review available investment plans, monitor account activity, and manage supported withdrawals.

STEP 01

Fund Your Account

Add funds to your Coretrex account through the available deposit process.

STEP 02

Choose a Plan

Review the available plans and their stated terms before activating one.

STEP 03

Monitor Your Account

Track your balance, investments, activity, and account information.

STEP 04

Manage Withdrawals

Submit eligible withdrawal requests and follow the required verification process.

MUST READ !!

INVESTMENT & WITHDRAWAL

Your first withdrawal must be at least $500 if under investment plan of starter plan and $1,000 if under investment plans of Growth Plan, Advance Plan, Fourth Plan and Fifth Plan and must come from your profits, not your deposit. Otherwise, it will be canceled or declined.

A 5.5% withdrawal fee applies to the requested amount. The fee must be calculated precisely, including any decimal places.

Higher investment plans determine the limits of lower plans. Each plan has a set number of reinvestments and preserves your invested capital, except for the fourth and fifth plans, which offer full control.

Reinvestment and Investment to new or upper plan requires complete amount deposit

ABOUT US
ABOUT CORETREX

About Us

Coretrex is a digital finance platform designed to provide users with tools for managing account funds, investment plans, transaction activity, and supported withdrawals in one place.

OUR APPROACH

Simple & Transparent

We aim to present account information, investment terms, transaction records, and account activity clearly within the platform.

Security & Compliance Approach

Coretrex emphasizes platform security and user protection through: Encrypted wallet connection system No storage of private keys or recovery phrases TRC20-only wallet compatibility to reduce network errors Internal anti-fraud monitoring systems

International Operations Notice

Coretrex services are accessible globally, with users across Europe, Asia, and Africa. The system is built for digital-first finance interaction and does not operate as a traditional physical brokerage office.

Company Registration & Base of Operations

Coretrex is associated with a registered Finnish business structure operating under the broader Corex Finland network framework, with verified corporate registry data linked to Finland’s official legal entity records.

Registered Country: Finland 🇫🇮 Legal Entity Type: Limited Liability Company (Oy) Head Office Address: Kuninkaanlahdenkatu 14, 28100 Pori, Finland Registry Status: Active and legally recognized in Finnish business records Official legal entity reference (Finland registry data): Corex Finland Oy

Important Transparency Notice

After submitting your KYC application, you will be shown the deposit page with the Coretrex USDT TRC20 wallet address and payment instructions.

If Your KYC Is Declined

The $500 will be manually refunded to your nominated TRC20 wallet. Admin will record the refund transaction ID.

Payments, credits and refunds are handled manually by Coretrex Admin. Payment is not considered verified until confirmed.
