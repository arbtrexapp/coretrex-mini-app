ABOUT US
ABOUT CORETREX

About Us

Coretrex is a digital finance platform designed to provide users with tools for managing account funds, investment plans, transaction activity, and supported withdrawals in one place.

OUR APPROACH

Simple & Transparent

We aim to present account information, investment terms, transaction records, and account activity clearly within the platform.

Security & Compliance Approach

Coretrex emphasizes platform security and user protection through: Encrypted wallet connection system No storage of private keys or recovery phrases TRC20-only wallet compatibility to reduce network errors Internal anti-fraud monitoring systems

International Operations Notice

Coretrex services are accessible globally, with users across Europe, Asia, and Africa. The system is built for digital-first finance interaction and does not operate as a traditional physical brokerage office.

Company Registration & Base of Operations

Coretrex is associated with a registered Finnish business structure operating under the broader Corex Finland network framework, with verified corporate registry data linked to Finland’s official legal entity records.

Registered Country: Finland 🇫🇮 Legal Entity Type: Limited Liability Company (Oy) Head Office Address: Kuninkaanlahdenkatu 14, 28100 Pori, Finland Registry Status: Active and legally recognized in Finnish business records Official legal entity reference (Finland registry data): Corex Finland Oy

Important Transparency Notice
